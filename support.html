Support

App does not start

Confirm you are using the latest PulseSphere test build. Restart the app and the phone. During Expo testing, update Expo Go and rescan the project QR code.

Camera or gallery

Open Android or iOS app settings and allow camera/photo access for PulseSphere. Images stay on the device.

Voice playback

Check media volume and ensure an Arabic or English text-to-speech voice is installed in the phone settings.

Report a problem

Open an issue in the PulseSphere repository and include the phone model, OS version, and steps that caused the problem. Never include passwords or private backup data.
