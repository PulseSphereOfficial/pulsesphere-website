Privacy Policy

Effective: September 15, 2026

Local-first data

PulseSphere stores assistant conversations, tasks, notes, preferences, and visual memories locally on the user's device. The current mobile app does not require an account to use these features.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo-library access is used only when the user chooses to create a visual memory. Selected images are compressed and stored locally. PulseSphere does not upload them to its servers.

Authentication

The optional website login uses Firebase Authentication. Firebase may process the email address and authentication information according to Google's privacy terms. PulseSphere does not sell personal data.

User control

Users can delete chats, notes, tasks, images, or all local app data from the app. Text data can be exported and restored by the user.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support page.
